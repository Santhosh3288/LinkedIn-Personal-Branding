A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Got Questions? Let's Clear Things Up.

1. What does a LinkedIn personal branding strategist actually do?
▼

A LinkedIn personal branding strategist helps you build a cohesive, authentic online presence that attracts the right opportunities. We work on your profile positioning, content strategy, and consistent execution so you can grow your influence and inbound leads without it consuming all your time.

2. How is this different from standard ghostwriting?
▼

Ghostwriting is about writing content that someone else publishes under their name. Personal branding strategy is about building a complete positioning system. We work together to define who you are, what you stand for, and how to communicate that authentically through your content and profile. It's partnership, not outsourcing.

3. I don't have much time. How much time do I need to invest each week?
▼

Most clients spend 2-4 hours per week on their LinkedIn presence, depending on their engagement preferences. The Management package is designed specifically for busy professionals who want consistency without the daily grind. We handle planning and scheduling; you approve content and engage when you have time.

4. Will the content actually sound like me or will it feel generic?
▼

This is exactly what we focus on. We spend time understanding your voice, perspective, and what makes you different. Every post is crafted to sound like you—with your language, your examples, and your perspective. Generic content doesn't work, and we won't create it.

5. What is included in the ₹1 LinkedIn Profile Audit?
▼

The audit includes a review of your headline, about section, content strategy, and historical engagement. You'll get a personalized 3-point action plan with immediate next steps. The exact scope and session duration are confirmed during booking.

6. How soon can I expect tangible results or inbound leads?
▼

Most clients see increased profile views within 4-6 weeks of consistent content. Meaningful inbound opportunities typically take 2-3 months, depending on your niche and existing network. LinkedIn rewards consistency, so patience and a clear strategy go hand-in-hand.

7. Do you also manage scheduling and engagement?
▼

Yes. Scheduling is included in the Management package. We coordinate timing, post content, and optimize for LinkedIn's algorithm. Direct engagement (comments, messages) remains your responsibility to keep it personal and authentic.

8. How do we get started?
▼

Start with the ₹1 LinkedIn Audit to get clear on where you stand and what your profile needs. From there, we can discuss whether Strategy, Scripting, Management, or a combination of services makes sense for you. There's no pressure—just clarity and next steps.
